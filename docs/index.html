Common questions

Are your perfumes authentic?

Yes. FRAGNIVO sells genuine branded fragrances, and decants are filled from original bottles.

What is a 10 ml decant?

A decant is a smaller amount of fragrance transferred from the original bottle into a separate spray atomiser. It lets you wear a scent over several days before you choose the full bottle.

How do I pay?

There is no payment on the website. We contact you to confirm your order, and you pay on delivery.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is charged separately. We will confirm the cost when we contact you.

Which perfumes do you sell in Sri Lanka?

We currently offer Rasasi Hawas for Him, Rasasi Hawas Fire, Afnan 9PM Rebel and French Avenue Vulcan Feu, each as a 10 ml decant or a 100 ml bottle.
